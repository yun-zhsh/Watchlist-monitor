JD.COM ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

Beijing, China—November 17, 2026—JD.com, Inc. (NASDAQ: JD and HKEX: 9618 (HKD counter) and 89618 (RMB counter), the “Company” or “JD.com”), a leading supply chain-based technology and service provider, today announced its unaudited financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

  •  

Net revenues were RMB309.6 billion (US$146.2 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 3.5% from the third quarter of 2025.

 

  •  

Income from operations was RMB5.2 billion (US$0.8 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss of RMB1.1 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Operating margin was 1.7% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to negative 0.4% for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP2 income from operations was RMB7.3 billion (US$1.1 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to RMB0.2 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin was 2.4% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 0.1% for the third quarter of 2025.

 

  •  

Net income attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders was RMB6.5 billion (US$1.0 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to RMB5.3 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Net margin attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders was 2.1% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 1.8% for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders was RMB8.3 billion (US$1.2 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to RMB5.8 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net margin attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders was 2.7% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 1.9% for the third quarter of 2025.

 

  •  

Diluted net income per ADS3 increased by 35.4% to RMB4.59 (US$0.68) for the third quarter of 2026 from RMB3.39 for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP diluted net income per ADS increased by 57.6% to RMB5.88 (US$0.88) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to RMB3.73 for the third quarter of 2025.

 

  •  

JD Retail reported income from operations of RMB15.1 billion (US$2.2 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to RMB14.8 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Operating margin of JD Retail was 5.8% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 5.9% for the third quarter of 2025.

“We delivered solid results in the third quarter, returning to positive revenue growth while accelerating profit growth,” said Sandy Xu, Chief Executive Officer of JD.com. “This performance was underpinned by the resilience of JD Retail and continued loss reduction in New Businesses. We also expanded our user base in the quarter alongside a notable year-on-year reduction in marketing expenses, reflecting our focus on operating efficiency. Beyond the healthy fundamentals of our existing operations, we were encouraged by the successful closing of the CECONOMY acquisition, which marks an important step forward in our global retail and logistics expansion. Overall, as we deepen our roots in existing markets while extending our international reach, we are establishing a solid foundation for long-term, high-quality growth.”

“In the third quarter, our total revenues were up 3.5% year-on-year, and non-GAAP net profit attributable to ordinary shareholders rose 44% year-on-year to RMB8.3 billion, with growth in both metrics accelerating from the previous quarter,” said Ian Su Shan, Chief Financial Officer of JD.com. “JD Retail remained our steady anchor, keeping operating margin largely stable against last year’s high base. New Businesses delivered meaningful loss reductions both year-on-year and sequentially in the quarter, driven by JD Food Delivery and Jingxi. On the international front, Joybuy is building solid momentum, which we believe will be further strengthened by potential synergies with CECONOMY. Going forward, our heightened focus on efficiency and financial discipline reinforces our confidence in driving long-term, high-quality expansion and shareholder value creation.”

Business Highlights

 

  •  

JD Retail:

JD.com further improved users’ shopping experiences through AI with the launch of JD APP version 16.0 and the debut of “Dongdong”, an AI-powered shopping assistant, on September 9. Dongdong accurately interprets complex consumer intents and maps them directly to our vast catalog of products and services. This further embeds AI capabilities across the entire shopping journey, including demand generation, product selection, purchasing decisions, and fulfillment services.

On September 28, JD.com opened our first 101HOME in Jing’an District, Shanghai. As a key step in JD.com’s expansion into offline premium lifestyle retail and deepening omnichannel integration, 101HOME leverages JD.com’s super supply chain capabilities to bring together curated furniture and home products, smart appliances, whole-house customization, and furnishing services under one roof. It has attracted many well-known international brands to make their store debuts in China.

JD.com continued to enrich its brand portfolio and build consumer mindshare in the apparel category. On September 16, Inditex Group, the global fashion powerhouse, officially launched flagship stores for both ZARA and ZARA HOME on JD.com. The launch covers menswear, womenswear, kidswear, footwear, bags, accessories, as well as home goods, with new arrivals synchronized with global releases. JD.com will fully integrate its capabilities in supply chain, logistics, technology and digital operations to collaborate with ZARA to unlock broader opportunities in the Chinese market, enhancing the consumer shopping experience and delivering more high-quality fashion choices.

  •  

JD Logistics:

During the launch of the iPhone 18 Pro lineup in September 2026, JD Logistics (“JDL”) capitalized on its full-spectrum fulfillment network which features “B2C warehousing and distribution plus on-demand delivery.” Powered by algorithm-driven sales forecasting and inventory planning, JDL positioned inventory in advance to its nationwide warehouses and front warehouses. Upon launch, it achieved “minute-level” delivery in core cities while providing broad coverage of counties and districts across China, smoothly handling surging order volumes. This service capability has also been extended to overseas markets. JDL provided fulfillment support for Joybuy, JD.com’s European online retail business, supporting its launch of the iPhone 18 Pro lineup and trade-in service. In areas covered by the “Double 11” (211) delivery service, customers can receive their iPhone 18 Pro on the same day they order.

 

  •  

JD Health:

JD Health (“JDH”) continued to strengthen its pharmaceutical and healthcare supply chain capabilities. In the third quarter of 2026, over 50 new drugs made their platform debuts on JDH, including Yangtze River Pharmaceutical’s innovative drug Bailemian®, GeneScience Pharmaceuticals’ domestic gout innovation Jinbeixin®, and Hengrui Pharma’s new drug Hengyi®. As of the third quarter of 2026, over 110 pharmaceutical companies had chosen JDH as their launch channel for new products.

 

  •  

JD Industrials:

In the third quarter of 2026, JD Industrials (“JDI”) continued to strengthen its full-chain AI technology capabilities and expand its industrial ecosystem development. JoyIndustrial 2.0, JDI’s industrial AI model, was certified in the first tier both domestically and internationally by the China Academy of Industrial Internet. Building on the JoyIndustrial 2.0 foundation, JDI launched “Industrial Insight”, an innovative AI-powered computer-aided design (CAD) application that generates 3D models of machinable components based on natural language instructions or reference images and automatically completes a bill of materials (BOM) breakdown, integrating the entire process from industrial design and component selection to procurement. This helps enterprises shorten development cycles for customized components and lower barriers to innovation.

 

  •  

New Businesses:

In the third quarter of 2026, JD.com achieved steady progress in new businesses. JD Food Delivery continued to see healthy development, while overall quarterly investment narrowed significantly year-on-year, mainly driven by enhanced operational efficiency and revenue diversification. Joybuy established partnerships with renowned brands including Bose, Danone, Dyson, LG IT, Sandisk, SharkNinja, and more, and has launched services including trade-in programs and the JoyCare product protection and insurance service for tech products. Our signature “Double 11” (211) delivery service offered by Joybuy is available in more than 80 towns and cities in Europe. Driven by high-quality product offerings and exceptional service, Joybuy has achieved robust growth across multiple metrics, including GMV and user base, since its official launch.

On November 12, 2026, JD.com completed its voluntary public takeover offer for CECONOMY AG (“CECONOMY”) (XETRA: CEC), the parent company of leading European consumer electronics retailer MediaMarktSaturn, acquiring 59.8% of its share capital and voting rights with total consideration of EUR1,335 million. As a result, CECONOMY has become a subsidiary of JD.com, and its financial results have been consolidated into JD.com’s consolidated financial statements since November 12, 2026. Together with the 25.3% stake retained by JD.com’s partner Convergenta, this acquisition brings our combined shareholding in CECONOMY to 85.2%. The transaction strengthens JD.com’s presence in Europe and establishes a strategic partnership with CECONOMY. By combining JD.com’s technology and logistics capabilities with CECONOMY’s established retail network and local expertise, the partnership aims to deliver a better experience for consumers in Europe.

Environmental, Social and Governance

As a testament to JD.com’s unwavering commitment to creating more jobs and making contribution to the society, the total personnel under the JD Ecosystem4 was over 900,000 as of September 30, 2026, including the Company’s employees, part-time staff and interns, as well as the personnel of the Company’s affiliates in the JD Ecosystem. The total expenditure for such human resources, together with the expenditure for external personnel who work for the JD Ecosystem, amounted to RMB171.3 billion for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Net Revenues. Net revenues increased by 3.5% to RMB309.6 billion (US$46.2 billion) for the third quarter of 2026 from RMB299.1 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Net product revenues increased by 3.1%, while net service revenues increased by 4.7% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to the third quarter of 2025.

Cost of Revenues. Cost of revenues increased by 2.0% to RMB253.6 billion (US$37.8 billion) for the third quarter of 2026 from RMB248.6 billion for the third quarter of 2025.

Fulfillment Expenses. Fulfillment expenses are related to retail businesses and primarily consist of expenses for operating and staffing fulfillment centers, customer service centers and physical stores, along with procurement and payment processing expenses. Fulfillment expenses increased by 7.4% to RMB23.6 billion (US$3.5 billion) for the third quarter of 2026 from RMB22.0 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Fulfillment expenses as a percentage of net revenues was 7.6% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 7.4% for the third quarter of 2025, as the Company continued to upgrade fulfillment capabilities and invest in human capital to enhance user experience while building the operational infrastructure for new business initiatives.

Marketing Expenses. Marketing expenses decreased by 26.2% to RMB15.5 billion (US$2.3 billion) for the third quarter of 2026 from RMB21.1 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Marketing expenses as a percentage of net revenues was 5.0% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 7.0% for the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to the optimization of promotional spending on new business initiatives.

Supplemental Information

The Company reports three reportable segments, JD Retail, JD Logistics, and New Businesses. JD Retail, which includes JD Health and JD Industrials, mainly engages in online retail, online marketplace and marketing services in China. JD Logistics includes both internal and external logistics businesses. New Businesses mainly include JD Food Delivery, Jingxi, JD Property and overseas businesses.

On October 31, 2025, JD Logistics acquired the local on-demand delivery services business previously under the New Businesses. Following this, JD Logistics integrated its full-time and crowdsourced delivery capabilities to enhance its service quality. Effective January 2026, JD Logistics’s on-demand delivery services shifted from serving the Company’s internal business segments, such as JD Food Delivery, to directly serving third-party merchants on the Company’s platform. Consequently, since the first quarter of 2026, JD Logistics’s internal revenues from on-demand delivery services declined as part of them were reclassified into external revenues, and New Businesses’ external on-demand delivery service revenues reflected a corresponding decrease.

Non-GAAP Measures

In evaluating the business, the Company considers and uses non-GAAP measures, such as non-GAAP income/(loss) from operations, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders, non-GAAP net margin attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders, free cash flow, non-GAAP EBITDA, non-GAAP EBITDA margin, non-GAAP net income/(loss) per share and non-GAAP net income/(loss) per ADS, as supplemental measures to review and assess operating performance. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“U.S. GAAP”). The Company defines non-GAAP income/(loss) from operations as income/(loss) from operations excluding share-based compensation, amortization of intangible assets resulting from assets and business acquisitions, effects of business cooperation arrangements, gain on sale of development properties, impairment of goodwill and long-lived assets and the Fine imposed by the SAMR. The Company defines non-GAAP net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders as net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders excluding share-based compensation, amortization of intangible assets resulting from assets and business acquisitions, effects of business cooperation arrangements and non-compete agreements, gain/(loss) on disposals/deemed disposals of investments, reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, gain/(loss) from fair value change of long-term investments and derivatives, impairment of goodwill, long-lived assets and investments, gain on sale of development properties, the Fine imposed by the SAMR, and tax effects on non-GAAP adjustments. The Company defines free cash flow as operating cash flow adjusting the impact from consumer financing receivables included in the operating cash flow and capital expenditures, net of related sales proceeds. Capital expenditures include purchase of property, equipment and software, cash paid for construction in progress, purchase of intangible assets, land use rights and asset acquisitions. The Company defines non-GAAP EBITDA as non-GAAP income/(loss) from operations plus depreciation and amortization excluding amortization of intangible assets resulting from assets and business acquisitions. Non-GAAP basic net income/(loss) per share is calculated by dividing non-GAAP net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the periods. Non-GAAP diluted net income/(loss) per share is calculated by dividing non-GAAP net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders by the weighted average number of ordinary shares and dilutive potential ordinary shares outstanding during the periods, including the dilutive effects of share-based awards as determined under the treasury stock method and convertible senior notes. Non-GAAP net income/(loss) per ADS is equal to non-GAAP net income/(loss) per share multiplied by two.

The Company presents these non-GAAP financial measures because they are used by management to evaluate operating performance and formulate business plans. Non-GAAP income/(loss) from operations, non-GAAP net income/(loss) attributable to the Company’s ordinary shareholders and non-GAAP EBITDA reflect the Company’s ongoing business operations in a manner that allows more meaningful period-to-period comparisons. Free cash flow enables management to assess liquidity and cash flow while taking into account the impact from consumer financing receivables included in the operating cash flow and the demands that the expansion of fulfillment infrastructure and technology platform has placed on financial resources. The Company believes that the use of the non-GAAP financial measures facilitates investors to understand and evaluate the Company’s current operating performance and future prospects in the same manner as management does, if they so choose. The Company also believes that the non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information to both management and investors by excluding certain expenses, gain/loss and other items that are not expected to result in future cash payments or that are non-recurring in nature or may not be indicative of the Company’s core operating results and business outlook.

The non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. The Company’s non-GAAP financial measures do not reflect all items of income and expense that affect the Company’s operations or not represent the residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures. Further, these non-GAAP measures may differ from the non-GAAP information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. The Company compensates for these limitations by reconciling the non-GAAP financial measures to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measure, all of which should be considered when evaluating performance. The Company encourages you to review the Company’s financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.
