THIRD QUARTER 2019 RESULTS

REPORTED BY AMPHENOL CORPORATION

 

Wallingford, Connecticut.  October 23, 2019.  Amphenol Corporation (NYSE: APH) reported today GAAP diluted Earnings Per Share (“EPS”) for the third quarter 2019 of $0.92 compared to $1.01 for the comparable 2018 period.  GAAP diluted EPS for the third quarter 2019 included refinancing-related costs of approximately $14 million ($0.04 per share) associated with the early extinguishment of debt during the quarter, partially offset by an excess tax benefit of approximately $2 million ($0.01 per share) related to stock options exercised during the quarter.  Third quarter 2018 GAAP diluted EPS included an excess tax benefit of approximately $7 million ($0.02 per share) related to stock options exercised during the quarter. Excluding the effect of these items, Adjusted Diluted EPS1 for the third quarter 2019 was $0.95 compared to $0.99 for the third quarter 2018. 

 

Sales for the third quarter 2019 were $2.101 billion compared to $2.129 billion for the comparable 2018 period. Currency translation had the effect of decreasing sales by approximately $24 million in the third quarter 2019 compared to the 2018 period.

 

For the nine months ended September 30, 2019, GAAP Diluted EPS was $2.72, compared to $2.76 for the comparable 2018 period.  GAAP Diluted EPS for the nine months ended September 30, 2019, included acquisition-related costs of approximately $25 million ($0.07 per share) and refinancing-related costs associated with the early extinguishment of debt during the period of approximately $14 million ($0.04 per share), partially offset by an excess tax benefit of approximately $21 million ($0.07 per share) related to stock options exercised during the period.  The comparable 2018 period included an excess tax benefit of approximately $14 million ($0.04 per share) related to stock options exercised during the period.  Excluding the effect of these items, Adjusted Diluted EPS for the nine months ended September 30, 2019 and 2018 was $2.76 and $2.72, respectively.

 

Sales for the nine months ended September 30, 2019 were $6.074 billion compared to $5.977 billion for the 2018 period.  Currency translation had the effect of decreasing sales by approximately $112 million for the first nine months of 2019 compared to the 2018 period.

 

Amphenol President and Chief Executive Officer, R. Adam Norwitt, stated, “We are pleased to close the third quarter 2019 with both sales and Adjusted Diluted EPS exceeding the high end of our guidance, reaching $2.101 billion and $0.95, respectively.  Compared to the third quarter 2018, sales decreased 1% as a result of moderations in the industrial, automotive and communications-related markets, offset in part by strong growth in the military and commercial air markets as well as contributions from the Company’s acquisition program.  We are proud that the Company continues to execute well amidst a very uncertain environment, with Adjusted Operating Margins of 19.7% in the third quarter.”

 

“The Company continues to expand its growth opportunities through a deep commitment to developing enabling technologies for customers in all markets, an ongoing strategy of market and geographic diversification and an active and successful acquisition program.  As part of that program, we are excited to have recently closed two new acquisitions, which collectively generate annual sales of approximately $60 million.  In the last several weeks, the Company acquired XGiga Communication Technology Co., Ltd. (“XGiga”) as well as Cablescan Limited (“Cablescan”). Based in China, XGiga designs and manufactures active fiber optic interconnect components used primarily in the communications infrastructure markets. Based in the United Kingdom, Cablescan designs and manufactures high-technology interconnect assemblies for the military and commercial aerospace markets. These two acquisitions are highly complementary to the Company’s existing global capabilities and enhance our product offerings across these end markets, while bringing two strong management teams into the Amphenol family.”

 

“Operating cash flow in the quarter was a very strong $412 million, another confirmation of the quality of the Company’s earnings.  The Company continues to deploy its financial strength in a variety of ways to increase shareholder value.  To that end, the Company

purchased 1.7 million shares of its common stock during the quarter for approximately $150 million and paid dividends of approximately $68 million.”

 

“As we look ahead, the overall demand environment continues to reflect a heightened level of uncertainty in the global economy.  Given the current demand environment, together with contributions from the acquisitions just completed, we expect fourth quarter 2019 sales to be in the range of $1.960 billion to $2.000 billion and Adjusted Diluted EPS in the range of $0.89 to $0.91.  For the full year 2019, we now expect sales in the range of $8.035 billion to $8.075 billion, a decrease of 2% over 2018, and Adjusted Diluted EPS to be in the range of $3.65 to $3.67, which is a decrease of 3% over 2018.  This compares to our prior full-year 2019 guidance for sales in the range of $7.920 billion to $8.000 billion and Adjusted Diluted EPS in the range of $3.56 to $3.60.”

 

“While the current demand environment continues to be very challenging, we remain confident in the platform of strength that has been created by the Company’s consistent and strong performance.  The electronics revolution is creating exciting long-term growth opportunities for Amphenol across each of our diversified end markets, with customers driving their products and networks to achieve ever higher levels of performance.  We believe these opportunities will enable a long-term increase in demand for our expanded range of high-technology interconnect, sensor and antenna products.  Our ongoing actions to leverage our competitive advantages and create sustained financial strength, as well as our initiatives to expand our high-technology product offerings, both organically and through our acquisition program, have created an excellent base for future performance.  I am confident in the ability of our outstanding, entrepreneurial management team to dynamically adjust to the always changing environment, to continue to generate strong profitability and to further capitalize on the many opportunities to expand our market position regardless of the underlying demand environment.”

Amphenol Corporation is one of the world’s largest designers, manufacturers and marketers of electrical, electronic and fiber optic connectors, interconnect systems, antennas, sensors and sensor-based products and coaxial and high-speed specialty cable.  Amphenol designs, manufactures and assembles its products at facilities in the Americas, Europe, Asia, Australia and Africa and sells its products through its own global sales force, independent representatives and a global network of electronics distributors.  Amphenol has a diversified presence as a leader in high-growth areas of the interconnect market including: Automotive, Broadband Communications, Commercial Aerospace, Industrial, Information Technology and Data Communications, Military, Mobile Devices and Mobile Networks.
