PDD Holdings Announces Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

 

DUBLIN and SHANGHAI, Nov. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – PDD Holdings Inc. (“PDD Holdings” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: PDD), today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

·Total revenues in the quarter were RMB108.3 billion (US$116.2 billion), compared with RMB108.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

·Operating profit in the quarter was RMB31.2 billion (US$4.6 billion). Non-GAAP2 operating profit in the quarter was RMB32.6 billion (US$4.9 billion).

 

·Net income attributable to ordinary shareholders in the quarter was RMB29.5 billion (US$4.4 billion), compared with RMB29.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income attributable to ordinary shareholders in the quarter was RMB31.0 billion (US$4.6 billion), compared with RMB31.4 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

“During the past quarter, we continued to deepen our support for the merchant ecosystem, helping merchants develop better products and reach new consumers,” said Mr. Jiazhen Zhao, Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer of PDD Holdings. “We see significant potential to build on this progress and will continue investing in the tools and services that help our merchants grow.”

 

“As our global business adapts to an evolving trade and regulatory environment, we remain committed to deepening our supply chain investments,” said Mr. Lei Chen, Co-Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer of PDD Holdings. “By helping merchants serve consumers with greater efficiency and reliability, we are building a more resilient platform that delivers long-term value for the local communities we serve.”

 

“We remain in a strategic investment phase, focused on strengthening supply chain capabilities and supporting our merchants,” said Ms. Jun Liu, VP of Finance of PDD Holdings. “We will maintain investment discipline while funding initiatives that support a healthy merchant ecosystem and strengthen our platform’s long-term competitiveness.”

Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

 

Total revenues were RMB108.3 billion (US$16.2 billion), compared with RMB108.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

·Revenues from online marketing services and others were RMB59.8 billion (US$8.9 billion), an increase of 12% from RMB53.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

·Revenues from transaction services were RMB48.5 billion (US$7.2 billion), a decrease of 12% from RMB54.9 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

Total costs of revenues were RMB45.2 billion (US$6.7 billion), compared with RMB46.8 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

Total operating expenses were RMB31.9 billion (US$4.8 billion), a decrease of 12% from RMB36.4 billion in the same quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the decrease in sales and marketing expenses.

 

·Sales and marketing expenses were RMB25.3 billion (US$3.8 billion), compared with RMB30.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

·General and administrative expenses were RMB1.6 billion (US$236 million), compared with RMB1.8 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

 

·Research and development expenses were RMB5.1 billion (US$755 million), compared with RMB4.3 billion in the same quarter of 2025.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

In evaluating the business, the Company considers and uses non-GAAP measures, such as non-GAAP operating profit, non-GAAP net income attributable to ordinary shareholders, non-GAAP diluted earnings per ordinary share, and non-GAAP diluted earnings per ADS, as supplemental measures to review and assess operating performance. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“U.S. GAAP”). The Company’s non-GAAP financial measures exclude the impact of share-based compensation expenses.

 

The Company presents these non-GAAP financial measures because they are used by management to evaluate operating performance and formulate business plans. The Company believes that the non-GAAP financial measures help identify underlying trends in its business by excluding the impact of share-based compensation expenses, which are non-cash charges. The Company also believes that the non-GAAP financial measures may provide further information about the Company’s results of operations, and enhance the overall understanding of the Company’s past performance and future prospects.

 

The Company’s non-GAAP financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. These non-GAAP financial measures do not reflect all items of income and expenses that affect the Company’s operations and do not represent the residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures. Further, these non-GAAP measures may differ from the non-GAAP information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. The Company compensates for these limitations by reconciling the non-GAAP financial measures to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measure, all of which should be considered when evaluating performance. The Company encourages you to review the Company’s financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.

 

For more information on the non-GAAP financial measures, please see the table captioned “Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures to the Most Directly Comparable GAAP Measures” set forth at the end of this press release.
