The board of directors (the “Board”) of Alibaba Group Holding Limited (“Alibaba Group” or the “company”) is pleased to announce that the unaudited consolidated results of the company, its subsidiaries and consolidated entities (the “Group”) for the three months and the six months ended September 30, 2026. The Group’s unaudited consolidated financial statements have been prepared under accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“U.S. GAAP”) and reviewed by the audit committee (the “Audit Committee”) of the Board.

 

In this results announcement, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the company and where the context otherwise requires, the Group.

BUSINESS AND STRATEGIC UPDATES

 

Alibaba E-commerce Group

 

During the quarter, we sustained revenue stability with CMR decreasing 5% year-over-year and growing 1% on a like-for-like basis if the contra revenue impact from the new business development program were excluded. Adjusted EBITA of the group achieved 243% year-over-year growth this quarter, primarily driven by a significant loss reduction in the China Quick Commerce business. In addition, our China E-commerce business contributed to stable operating profit and the Global Wholesale business delivered strong profits, while we reduced losses in the International E-commerce business.

 

We have continued to advance our strategic priorities of unlocking significant synergies across our domestic and cross-border businesses, and deepening the integration of AI capabilities across our e-commerce platforms. Recent progress includes the integration of management across our domestic and international wholesale businesses, as well as the launch of QwenWork E-commerce for merchants, an AI product powered by our agentic AI capabilities, in October.

 

QwenWork E-commerce is integrated into QwenWork, our one-stop AI agent platform, and is designed to automate end-to-end merchant workflows, including store operations, business analytics, advertising and customer service. Leveraging our existing merchant tools, it connects directly to Taobao and Tmall data and will further support data access across other platforms, enabling more seamless cross-platform operations. In addition, by leveraging Qwen model family’s advanced multimodal capabilities, we are able to better understand complex consumer needs and deliver more relevant product recommendations with knowledge-based reasoning. These enhanced capabilities have driven a notable increase in general knowledge-related queries, creating more opportunities to identify latent consumer demand, facilitate product discovery, and drive sales conversion.

 

In our China Quick Commerce business, Taobao Instant Commerce continued to improve its unit economics quarter-over-quarter while maintaining market share. This reflected higher average order value, as we further improved the order mix through an increasing focus on high-value food and non-food categories. Meanwhile, Freshippo maintained its robust growth across online and offline channels, driven by deeper collaboration with Taobao Instant Commerce and the expansion of its multi-format store network that offers differentiated products to diverse demographics.

 

The number of 88VIP members, our highest spending consumer group, continued to increase by double digits year-over-year to approximately 66 million as of September 30, 2026. The ARPU of new 88VIP members, defined as those who joined the membership program within the past year, increased significantly year-over-year during the 11.11 Global Shopping Festival. We remain focused on the growth and retention of 88VIP members through enhanced value proposition to our most valued customers.

 

Xianyu is China’s leading consumer-to-consumer community and marketplace for idle goods. It makes buying and selling more convenient for consumers and small businesses through AI-powered features including smart pricing, automated product listing descriptions and precise buyer targeting, as well as consignment services. This quarter, Xianyu continued its strong growth momentum, with daily active users and paid orders up 23% and 29% year-over-year, respectively.

AI Cloud and Compute Services

 

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue from AI Cloud and Compute Services was RMB60,973 million (US$9,094 million), a 53% increase from the same quarter last year. The growth of revenue from external customers accelerated to 64% year-over-year. The growth of recurring revenue from external customers, which excludes compute equipment solutions revenue, also accelerated to 67% year-over-year. In particular, AI-related product revenue continued to show strong momentum, achieving RMB17,918 million (US$2,673 million) and delivering the thirteenth consecutive quarter of triple-digit year-over-year growth. The strong growth reflected broad-based demand for our AI + Cloud services. Beyond training and inference workloads at AI labs, customers across the internet, automotive, embodied AI, financial services and biotechnology sectors rapidly expanded their use of our cloud services to support innovation and grow their business.

 

Cloud

 

Alibaba Cloud continued to strengthen our market-leading full-stack AI capabilities, as demonstrated by the comprehensive upgrades unveiled at the Apsara Conference in September, spanning proprietary chips, supernode architecture, agent-native cloud infrastructure, AI models and agents. These advancements across our co-designed and integrated stack have accelerated enterprise adoption and deeper integration into core business workflows, enabling us to capitalize on increasingly diverse demand across industries.

 

Our ability to empower enterprise AI adoption has earned notable recognition in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Strategic Cloud Platform Services, a report that comprehensively evaluates how cloud service providers support the entire enterprise cloud lifecycle. Alibaba Cloud was named a Leader in the highest quadrant, making it the first and only Asia-Pacific provider to receive this distinction.

 

Chip Design – T-Head Semiconductor (“T-Head”)

 

By rapidly advancing its portfolio of proprietary chips and software, T-Head further strengthened the infrastructure foundation underpinning our AI capabilities.

 

At the Apsara Conference in September, we unveiled the Zhenwu V900, our next-generation AI chip for training and inference, with commercial release scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. Built on our proprietary parallel computing architecture, the Zhenwu V900 delivers significant performance improvements over its predecessor, the Zhenwu M890. We also open-sourced T-Head SAIL, the software stack for Zhenwu chips, to simplify application migration, expand developer adoption, and strengthen compatibility with mainstream AI frameworks.

 

In addition, we launched the Zhenwu M890 supernode instance in August, marking the introduction of China’s first supernode architecture capable of supporting inference for models with more than two trillion parameters. At the Apsara Conference, we also showcased our next-generation supernode product, which integrates the Zhenwu V900 with other proprietary interconnect, networking, and storage chips, and is scheduled for launch in the first quarter of 2027.

AI Labs and Applications

 

Model

 

We continued to advance our comprehensive model portfolio, with a primary focus on foundation models, while enhancing capabilities across video, audio, image and music. This demonstrates our industry leadership and rapid pace of iteration.

 

Recently, we launched Qwen4-Max, built on the new mixture-of-experts architecture with 2.4 trillion backbone parameters and native visual capabilities. Qwen4-Max advances productivity and token efficiency, delivering stronger long-horizon task execution across industries, including manufacturing, game development, healthcare, and frontier scientific research.

 

QwenWork

 

Our agentic platform QwenWork continued to gain traction across industries, driven by ongoing enhancements to its enterprise capabilities. At the Apsara Conference in September, we launched Enterprise Context, a solution that continuously organizes and updates enterprise information and knowledge across company documents, meeting records and internal communications. It enables AI agents to access company-specific information and execute tasks based on organizational knowledge and workflows.

 

Qwen App

 

Qwen app for individual users now provides personalized assistance for tasks across more than 20 service categories, including shopping, local services, travel, work, learning, personal finance and health, leveraging Alibaba’s ecosystem and third-party partnerships. In October, we launched Qwen Go!, a cloud-based personal agent embedded within the Qwen app. Qwen Go! autonomously executes multi-step tasks across platforms, while retaining contextual information and user preferences over time to deliver increasingly personalized assistance across everyday activities.

 

All Others

 

Our store guide and mapping platform Amap offers consumers one-stop access to services such as navigation, to-destination services and ride-hailing. On October 1, 2026, the first day of National Day holiday in China, Amap recorded an all-time high of approximately 370 million peak daily active users. Leveraging Amap Street Stars, a feature that provides a trust-based rating system to support users’ consumption decisions, Amap has strengthened its mindshare in local services across consumption use cases, including dining, tourist attractions and seasonal activities.

 

Share Repurchases

 

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased a total of 17.95 million ordinary shares (equivalent to approximately 2.24 million ADSs) for a total of US$221 million. These purchases were made in the U.S. market under our share repurchase program.

 

Capital Expenditures

 

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, capital expenditures were RMB50,477 million (US$7,529 million), an increase of 60% compared to RMB31,501 million in the same quarter of 2025, due to continued investments in AI infrastructure to meet strong and accelerating customer demand. The significant year-over-year increase was due to an acceleration in scaling compute capacity, higher pricing of a broad range of chip components, an increase in CPU-compute capacity driven by anticipated growing customer adoption of AI agents, and fluctuations in procurement cycles.

 

In addition to training and inference demand from AI Labs, we continue to see robust demand from customers in China’s emerging innovation economy, including electric vehicles (EVs), embodied AI, and biotechnology. Scale is becoming an increasingly critical competitive advantage in cloud computing, providing us with orchestration flexibility, operating efficiency and cost advantages. By investing in infrastructure today, we aim to extend our scale leadership, strengthen our supply chain and reinforce our long-term competitive position.

FOREIGN EXCHANGE RISK

 

Foreign currency risk arises from future commercial transactions, recognized assets and liabilities and net investments in foreign operations. Although we operate businesses in different countries and regions, most of our revenue-generating transactions, and a majority of our expense-related transactions, are denominated in Renminbi, which is the functional currency of our major operating subsidiaries and the reporting currency of our financial statements. When considered appropriate, we enter into hedging activities with regard to exchange rate risk.

 

The value of the Renminbi against the U.S. dollar and other currencies may fluctuate and is affected by, among other things, changes in political and economic conditions and the foreign exchange policy adopted by the governments. It is difficult to predict how market forces or PRC or U.S. government policy may impact the exchange rate between the Renminbi and the U.S. dollar in the future. There remains significant international pressure on the PRC government to adopt a more flexible currency policy, which could result in greater fluctuations of the Renminbi against the U.S. dollar.

 

To the extent that we need to convert U.S. dollars into Renminbi for our operations, appreciation of the Renminbi against the U.S. dollar would reduce the Renminbi amount we receive from the conversion. Conversely, if we decide to convert Renminbi into U.S. dollars for the purpose of share repurchases, making payments for dividends on our ordinary shares or ADSs, servicing our outstanding debts, or for other business purposes, appreciation of the U.S. dollar against the Renminbi would reduce the U.S. dollar amounts available to us.
