5.INSURANCE

 

Gross Written Premiums (in R$ million)

 

 

Gross written premiums (GWP) refer to the total amount of premium income that XPs has written or sold during a particular reporting period before deductions for provisions, reinsurance and other expenses. This figure represents the total premiums that customers have agreed to pay for life insurance policies issued by the company or sold by the company and issued by third-party insurers, including both new policies and renewals. It is a crucial metric for assessing the total business volume of an insurance company or insurance broker within that period.

 

In 3Q26, Gross Written Premiums grew 17% YoY and 8% QoQ.

Discussion of Financial Results

 

Total Gross Revenue1

 

Gross revenue reached R$5.3 billion in 3Q26, reflecting an increase of 8% year-over-year and a 6% growth quarter-over-quarter.

 

Retail growth in the quarter was driven by equities, funds platform, new verticals, and other retail, which expanded at a rapid pace year over year. Despite the more challenging environment for capital markets, the Wholesale bank division also delivered sequential growth. Revenues were up by 13%, led by another consistent performance of our Corporate segment.

Retail revenue reached R$4,127 million in 3Q26, representing a 6% increase quarter-over-quarter and a 11% growth year-over-year.

 

Retail revenues were positively impacted by additional business days this quarter. Even with the lower ADTV of equities and futures in the third quarter, Equities revenue grew 6% sequentially, reaching approximately R$1.2 billion. Higher volumes of listed funds´ issuances during the third quarter partly explain this performance. On a yearly basis, Equities revenue increased 16%, reflecting improved client activity when compared to the same period of 2025. Following the same rationale, Funds Platform also posted a strong performance this quarter, growing 18% year over year and 3% sequentially.

 

Also, Retail annual performance benefited from stronger contributions from new verticals, especially Insurance and Credit, and different revenue lines included in Other Retail, like float, international platform and FX.

 

 


Take Rate

 

Annualized Retail Take Rate was 1.25% in 3Q26, 6bps higher QoQ and 1bp higher YoY.

Wholesale Banking

 

Taken together our Corporate, Issuer Services and Institutional businesses grew 13% year-over-year, with revenues totaling R$1,212 million in 3Q26.

 

Our Corporate segment continued presenting a good performance and posted another strong result with revenues growing 55% year over year and 4% sequentially. This expansion derives mainly from our credit and energy businesses coupled with derivatives. Our ability to cross-sell and deliver a broader set of solutions to our corporate clients continued to support our revenue growth.

Earnings Before Taxes

 

EBT was R$1,580 million in 3Q26, up 13% YoY and roughly stable QoQ. The EBT margin was 30.7%, 104 bps up versus the prior year and 130 bps lower quarter-over-quarter.

 

 

Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS1

 

In 3Q26, Adjusted Net Income reached R$1.4 billion, roughly stable QoQ and increasing 4% on a year-over-year comparison. Adjusted Basic EPS was R$2.73, 1% higher QoQ and 9% higher YoY. Adjusted Diluted EPS was R$2.69 for the quarter, 1% higher QoQ and 9% higher YoY.

 

 

Adjusted ROTE1,6 and Adjusted ROAE1,7

 

In 3Q26 our Adjusted Return on Equity (ROAE) reached 21.7%, while return on tangible equity (ROTE) was 26.2%. Considering we had capital generation without a corresponding distribution throughout the quarter, both metrics are lower sequentially and when compared to the same period last year.

 

 

Capital Management8

 

In 3Q26 our BIS Ratio was 20.3%, 6 bps higher QoQ and 86 bps lower YoY, while our total RWA was R$132.2 billion, with a 4% increase QoQ and 23% increase YoY. Our CET1 ratio remains at a comfortable level of 17.2%. During 3Q26, we executed share repurchases of approximately R$254 million. In addition, we are announcing a new buyback program of R$1.5 billion and new dividends in the amount of R$1.3 billion, to be paid still in 2026. Additionally, we will be cancelling approximately 11.7 million treasury shares, representing roughly 2.3% of our total outstanding shares — further reinforcing our commitment to disciplined capital allocation and returning value to our shareholders.
